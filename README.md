# 📸 snap - Decisions Without the Deliberation

## 🚀 What Is snap?

snap is a small but powerful tool that helps your computer make clear, reliable decisions from messy, unstructured information. Think of it as a lightning-fast translator that reads chaotic input—like a paragraph of text, a list of notes, or a pile of data—and instantly gives you a typed, organized answer. No long paragraphs generated, no rambling. Just one forward pass, and you get exactly what you need.

The best part? snap runs entirely on your computer. Your information never leaves your machine. It’s local, private, and always works the same way—deterministic, meaning you’ll get the same result every time you run the same input. No surprises, no guesswork.

## 🛠️ Who Is snap For?

snap is for anyone who deals with messy data and needs clear answers. That includes:

- **Students** organizing research notes into categories
- **Professionals** sorting customer feedback into structured buckets
- **Hobbyists** turning raw text into usable lists or tables
- **Curious users** who want a taste of what local AI can do, without cloud services

You do **not** need to be a programmer to use snap. The setup is simple, and once it’s running, you just give it input and it gives you structured output.

## 🤔 Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need an internet connection?

No. snap runs fully offline. Once you’ve downloaded it, you can unplug your internet and it will still work perfectly.

### Is my data private?

Absolutely. Since snap runs locally, your input never leaves your computer. There’s no cloud server, no analytics, no telemetry.

### What does "deterministic" mean?

It means snap gives you the same answer every time for the same input. There’s no randomness or variation. This makes it perfect for tasks where consistency matters, like data cleanup or sorting.

### What is "Jev-compatible"?

snap is built to work with the Jev ecosystem. If you use other Jev-based tools, snap fits right in. If you don’t, you can ignore this—snap still works completely on its own.

### Can I use snap for business-critical tasks?

Yes. Because snap is deterministic and runs locally, it’s suitable for production use cases where reliability and privacy are priorities.
